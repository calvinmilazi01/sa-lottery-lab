Hot and cold numbers

Hot and cold describe the past. Each view below puts the numbers next to what fair draws would produce, so you can see whether anything stands out beyond ordinary luck.

Forecast generator

Builds the 10 best-scoring combinations from past draws using the factors you weight below. Each number gets a forecast weight from its frequency in the look-back window, its recent form (recent draws count more, halving in weight every few draws) and how overdue it is. Candidate combinations are then scored on those weights, how often their pairs appeared together, how balanced they are, and how rarely other players pick them.

How would this generator have done?

Do the draws look random?

Each test compares the real draws with what a fair draw would produce. A p-value below 0.05 means the result would be unusual under fair draws. With many tests, expect about 1 in 20 to fall below 0.05 by chance alone.

Can any strategy beat a random pick?

Walk-forward test: for each draw, a strategy picks numbers using only the draws before it, then we count matches. The yellow line is the exact average for a random pick, and the shaded band shows where 95% of random pickers land over the same draws.

What is one board worth?

Expected value = sum of (probability × prize) over every division. The top prize is shared if others also win, so the calculator models other winners as a Poisson count based on how many boards are sold. Edit any prize in the table.

Pick numbers other players avoid

No pick can improve your chance of winning. What you can change is how many people you'd share a prize with. Many players choose birthdays (1–31), sequences, patterns and past winning numbers, so those combinations get split more ways when they come up. This picker draws random tickets and keeps those that score lowest on those habits.

Popularity score: +3 if every number is 31 or lower (+1.5 if only one is above 31), +2 for a run of 3 or more consecutive numbers, +5 for an evenly spaced set, +1 for 3 or more multiples of 7, +1 for many numbers ending in the same digit, +5 for any past winning combination and +2 for all but one number matching one. Lower is less popular. These weights are heuristics based on known player habits, not measured sales data.
